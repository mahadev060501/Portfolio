Engineering Background & Philosophy

How I approach network design, infrastructure stability, and systems administration.

My technical journey is grounded in continuous, practical configuration. Whether building multi-tier campus networks or deploying Linux server environments, my goal is always predictability, security, and performance. I focus heavily on structured addressing plans (VLSM/CIDR), fault-tolerant routing convergence (OSPF/EIGRP/BGP), and robust Layer 2 redundancy using EtherChannel and Rapid Spanning Tree.
